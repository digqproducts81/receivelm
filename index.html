Privacy policy

This policy explains what receiveLM collects when you use our website and our AI call-answering service, and how that information is used. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with GDPR and other applicable data protection laws.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, business name, email, and phone number.
  • Call audio, transcripts, and summaries generated when receiveLM answers a call on your behalf.
  • Basic usage data from this website, such as pages visited, used to understand how the site is used.

How we use it

  • To operate the call-answering service, including booking, routing, and summarizing calls.
  • To support and improve the accuracy of the AI agent over time.
  • To respond to inquiries and manage your account and billing.

Call recording

Calls handled by receiveLM may be recorded and transcribed so the service can carry out the requested task and so you can review what happened on a call. Where required by law, callers are notified that the call may be recorded.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with service providers who help us run receiveLM, such as hosting and telephony infrastructure, and only as needed to deliver the service.

Your GDPR rights

Your choices
