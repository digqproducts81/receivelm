Why we require approval

receiveLM's voice models are powerful. To keep the telephone network safe and to meet our legal obligations across jurisdictions (including TCPA in the US, GDPR in the EU, and equivalent laws elsewhere), we only onboard verified businesses that will use the service to answer inbound calls they are already authorised to receive. We do not permit outbound spam, unsolicited advertising, robocalls, or any illegal activity. Accounts found in violation are terminated immediately.
